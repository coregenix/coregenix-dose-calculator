i How the numbers are worked out

Containers to dispense

total volume (mL) = rate (mL/hr) × duration (hr)
containers        = total volume ÷ container size
dispense          = containers, rounded UP to a whole unit

Example: 100 mL/hr for 24 h = 2,400 mL. With 500 mL bottles that is 4.8, so 5 bottles are supplied (4 full and 1 used up to 400 mL, 100 mL left over). Minutes are converted to hours (90 min = 1.5 h). A rate in mL/min is multiplied by 60.

Pediatric maintenance fluid (Holliday–Segar)

Daily : 100 mL/kg for the first 10 kg
        + 50 mL/kg for the next 10 kg
        + 20 mL/kg for every kg above 20

Hourly (4-2-1 rule): 4 mL/kg/hr  (first 10 kg)
                     2 mL/kg/hr  (next 10 kg)
                     1 mL/kg/hr  (each kg above 20)

The hourly figure is the standard bedside rounding of the daily figure and is the rate used to count containers. Rates are capped at 100 mL/hr (2,400 mL/day). Neonates under 3 kg follow separate protocols.

Weight-based injections

dose (mg)       = weight (kg) × dose (mg/kg)
concentration   = vial strength (mg) ÷ vial volume (mL)
volume per dose = dose ÷ concentration
doses           = doses per day × hours ÷ 24   (rounded up)

Two vial counts are shown: single-dose vials (a fresh vial for every dose, remainder discarded) and multi-dose vial (the same vial can be re-used).

Drip rate

drops per minute = rate (mL/hr) × drop factor (gtt/mL) ÷ 60
